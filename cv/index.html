Selected experience

Data Architect / Principal Data Engineer

Raiffeisenbank Austria d.d., Croatia (RBA) | Sep 2025 - Feb 2026 | Project case study

  • Designed and implemented a lakehouse migration framework for an Oracle/PostgreSQL estate of approximately 4,300 ETL mappings and 1,800 landing tables. Defined Bronze/Silver/Gold layers and reusable dbt models targeting Spark and Delta Lake.
  • Developed automated reverse engineering of ODI, DataStage and PL/SQL flows, reconstructing table- and column-level lineage across more than 20 source systems. Produced dependency graphs, SQL traceability and structured metadata for downstream automation.
  • Built orchestration with nine specialised AI agents to translate legacy logic into dbt SQL. Ordered models by their dependencies, compiled and ran the SQL, compared results with production data and escalated failed checks.
  • Removed the need for approximately 340 separately stored data-quality tables by running quality checks within the new processing flow. Handed over reusable models and pipelines to the delivery team.
