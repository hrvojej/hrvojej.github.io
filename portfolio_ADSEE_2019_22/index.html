From video to analysable records

Prepared urban-street video data through extraction, filtering and sample selection. Developed machine/deep-learning workflows for objects, people and faces.

Converted detections into time-linked records with images and attributes. Built visual and tabular results and filters by video time, object type and other parameters, supporting further analysis and alerts tied to detections.

Experimental analysis

Explored model-generated estimates of age, gender and facial expression in research and teaching experiments. Incorporated the experimental outputs into time-linked visual and tabular analysis.

Used OpenCV, YOLO, Haar classifiers, DeepFace/FaceNet and the Python data-science stack, with PostgreSQL in the data layer.

Educational modules and dissemination

Created advanced educational modules and an extensive set of Jupyter notebooks covering preparation, detection, experimental attributes and visual/tabular analysis.

Set up a local Jupyter server on Algebra infrastructure for the prepared material. Used the notebooks to present and explain the project to students, joining research development and dissemination within the same ADSEE engagement.

The wider educational programme involved collaboration with five EU university data-science research departments and adaptation of content and teaching methods to different learner groups.
