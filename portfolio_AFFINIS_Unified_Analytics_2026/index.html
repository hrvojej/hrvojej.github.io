A separate analytics workstream within the eADR engagement

The work extended the analytical scope beyond the operational eADR application. I designed how the existing AFFINIS environment could accept the new eADR and eFTI/NSCP datasets and relate them to the existing Taho/SOTAH domain.

The Agency for Commercial Activities (AKD; Agencija za komercijalnu djelatnost) received the project documentation, analytical-validation requirements and resource-upgrade assessment. The recorded delivery concerns architecture and technical documentation.

From source data to shared analytics

Specified the ingestion interfaces, reference-data alignment, transformation rules and analytical datasets. Shared vehicle, driver and carrier identities connect records across domains without treating different business events as interchangeable.

Defined how Raw and Silver data in MinIO and Spark processing feed StarRocks analytical tables and Apache Superset reporting, with Dagster orchestration and traceability back to source records.

Reporting, validation and platform upgrade

Prepared KPI and dashboard specifications tied to business questions, access rules and metric definitions. The validation scope covers complete ingestion, correct transformations, source-to-report reconciliation and evidence for acceptance.

The upgrade assessment covers workload and capacity, storage, processing, analytical services, networking, operational components and verification after installation. These requirements support procurement and subsequent implementation.
