Data-warehousing seminar for AKD

Delivered a data-warehousing seminar for the Agency for Commercial Activities (AKD), alongside the SEOP engagement. The seminar combined an introduction to data warehouses and business intelligence with practical laboratory exercises.

  • Data-warehouse and business-intelligence architecture; warehouses, data marts, facts, dimensions and data granularity.
  • Building a data warehouse and working with a time dimension.
  • Columnstore indexes and their use in analytical workloads.
  • Extract, transform and load (ETL) procedures, including transactions and checkpoints.
  • Data extraction and reporting exercises.
