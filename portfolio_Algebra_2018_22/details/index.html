Role across engineering, research and education

Worked as a data engineer, data scientist, researcher and lecturer. The assignments ranged from collecting and restructuring institutional records to analytical warehouses, business reporting, computer-vision demonstrations and real-time electrical-grid software.

The role combined implementation with understanding the client’s problem and explaining the technology to students, project teams and other stakeholders.

Graduate teaching

Taught Disruptive Technologies on the Data Science master’s programme and E-business on the Software Engineering graduate programme. Connected technology choices with business needs, practical implementation and the way a solution would be used.

For Disruptive Technologies, authored project guidelines that allowed students to pursue machine learning, AI, data engineering or big-data engineering. Students had to investigate existing approaches, explain the problem and develop a project with a clear practical purpose.

Guiding a data-science project from the problem to a prototype

Structured the Disruptive Technologies project around CRISP-DM: business understanding, data understanding, preparation, modelling, evaluation and deployment or prototyping. The guidelines linked model development to the surrounding application and business process.

Included project planning, incremental GitHub commits, documentation of problems and solutions, and discussion with teaching staff. Encouraged a working minimum viable prototype and a realistic explanation of how an end user would use it.

  • Define the business objective and identify relevant data, including privacy or confidentiality constraints.
  • Explore and prepare the data: missing values, outliers, categorical variables and suitable model inputs.
  • Select and tune models, compare alternatives and evaluate them on held-out data; interpret the business consequences of errors.
  • Consider Python/scikit-learn and, for larger datasets, Spark ML within the project’s needs.
  • Explain prediction speed, request volume, CPU, memory, storage and network needs, and dependencies on other business processes.
  • Use a simple web prototype to demonstrate the result; the guidelines discussed Flask, Bootstrap and React as possible tools.

AI course design for international students

Contributed to English-language AI course material for Algebra’s international programme with the Australian Institute for Mobility Overseas. Co-developed a syllabus explaining AI technologies, their business and ethical implications, and practical ways to explore them.

The course design combined accessible technical explanations, business cases and demonstrations of cloud AI services. Topics included technology selection, adoption and cost, conversational interfaces, visual and cognitive services, advanced analytics and project proposals.

The teaching approach emphasised understanding what a technology does, where it can help and what would be required to use it in a real organisation.

Data engineering and analytical projects

At Hendal, developed Python and SQL extraction, cleaning and restructuring of survey records from four MySQL databases, followed by a warehouse foundation and Power BI/DAX reporting.

For Croatian labour-market analytics, integrated institutional data supplied through APIs, files, database exports and SFTP. Prepared Python/SQL processing, import and staging structures, warehouse loads and schedules.

Worked on AKD’s SEOP analytical platform and subsequently served as key expert and project lead for the data-warehouse stage. Also led a 2018 proposal for a Ministry of Agriculture visualisation system, covering data transformation and the analytical presentation of results.

Energy and EU research projects

For IRI2 / Neural Grid, developed software that compared PMU phasor angles, performed power-system calculations and triggered PLC actions. The project achieved a response below 50 ms, significantly faster than SCADA in this project.

In Erasmus ADSEE, developed computer-vision demonstrations and educational material connecting video preparation, detections, timestamped records and analytical views. Prepared Jupyter-based explanations and practical work for students.

Contributed to development and testing of the KnowING IPR web platform in the transnational Danube-region project. The detailed project pages explain the technical scope of these engagements.

Collaborative property-data exploration

Worked on a collaborative Njuškalo property-data prototype in 2019. Used Python, BeautifulSoup and pandas to collect and organise apartment advertisements, distinguishing promoted and regular listings and preparing structured fields for analysis.

The work addressed basic and extended listing attributes, timestamps, cleaning and extraction corrections. It provided a practical setting for discussing data quality, repeated collection and preparation of analytical datasets.

Explaining technology to a wider audience

Prepared the 2018 Changer Šibenik presentation “Do you talk with machines?”. Explained conversational AI, chatbots, speech synthesis, voice interfaces and examples of business use.

Across the teaching and project work, connected technical mechanisms with understandable examples and the decisions an organisation or learner needed to make.
