The modelling problem

Different marketing platforms described audiences, activity and campaigns through different structures. The work focused on bringing those sources into a consistent analytical representation.

Source and pipeline integration

Integrated data from Salesforce, Google AdWords, social platforms, Google Analytics, Pardot and email-campaign platforms. Worked through complex datasets, data flows and consistency between platforms.

Developed the ingestion, transformation and storage architecture. Used Matillion for ETL operations and delivery into the dbt/Snowflake environment.

Shared analytical structures

Led model development and implementation in dbt and Snowflake. Applied SQL transformations and source-domain knowledge to relate data from multiple channels.

Created a common analytical basis for marketing strategy and decisions by relating source data across channels through shared models and transformations.
