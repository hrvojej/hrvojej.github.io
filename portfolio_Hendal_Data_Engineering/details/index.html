Project in depth

Survey-data integration, warehousing and Power BI

Developed the data foundation and Power BI reporting for analysis of survey fieldwork. The project required combining research-specific tables from four MySQL databases into consistent analytical structures.

Client / context
Hendal · Algebra LAB
Period
During the Algebra / Algebra LAB engagement
My role

← Project overview

Consolidating the survey records

Each research project had its own source table. Built Python and SQL extraction, cleaning and restructuring to bring selected attributes into shared warehouse structures.

The documented source environment contained approximately ten million records and grew by about one million records per year. Implemented nightly enrichment and harmonisation for reporting.

Building the analytical layer

Developed the warehouse basis, Power BI dashboards and DAX calculations. Resolved complex extraction and restructuring tasks so data from different surveys could be compared consistently.

Fieldwork questions addressed by the requirements

Worked against a requested scope of up to 14 interactive reports or combinations of views. The questions included survey duration, time between successful interviews, interviewer and regional efficiency, contacts and refusals, dialler waiting and quota completion.

The requirements also covered supervision, interviewer compensation, participation in projects and instruction attendance. Some views depended on source information such as timestamps and recorded assessments being available.

Testing and knowledge transfer

Worked within the client’s infrastructure and testing requirements. The delivery framework included collaboration with source-system specialists and explanation of the analytical tools and their limitations.
