Consolidating a project-specific data estate

Each survey/research project had a separate table. Extracted selected attributes into common structures and implemented nightly enrichment and harmonisation.

Worked with a source environment containing approximately ten million records and growing by about one million records per year.

Data engineering and analytical delivery

Developed extraction, cleaning, combination and restructuring across four MySQL databases using Python and SQL. Built the analytical data basis, Power BI views and DAX calculations.

Fieldwork reporting requirements

The requested reporting scope covered up to 14 views or combinations over existing reports. It included survey duration and timing, interviewer/regional efficiency, contacts and refusals, dialler waiting, quota completion, supervision and interviewer compensation.

Developed the ETL, warehouse and Power BI components within the client’s infrastructure, testing and acceptance requirements.
