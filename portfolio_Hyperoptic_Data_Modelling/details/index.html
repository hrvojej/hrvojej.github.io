Telecom data models aligned with TM Forum SID / ODA

Analysed Hyperoptic’s telecom data structures and developed mappings to the TM Forum SID information model. The work connected business processes, existing ER diagrams, attribute-level mapping, UML models and practical integration prototypes, including the treatment of sites and geographic locations.

Connecting existing systems with a shared telecom model

Worked as Data Engineer and Data Modeler for Hyperoptic through Intuita. The practical challenge was to relate the concepts already represented in operational databases to a shared industry vocabulary and data model.

The work addressed existing structures and the processes they supported. I used SID and Open Digital Architecture as reference frameworks, then mapped the relevant client entities and relationships rather than assuming that an entire operational table belonged to one SID domain.

Starting from business processes and source data

Prepared a bottom-up mapping method that starts with a business process, breaks it into understandable activities and identifies the systems, tables and attributes involved. The procedure records participants, dependencies, constraints and source/destination responsibilities.

The proposed source inventory includes database type, schema, table, attributes and ownership. This creates a traceable route from a business activity to the data that implements it.

High-level and detailed activity, state or sequence diagrams provide different views of the same process. They complement the ER diagrams rather than replacing the actual database relationships.

Reverse engineering the relational structures

Worked with database DDL and ER diagrams in tools including MySQL Workbench and Sparx Enterprise Architect. Selected the relevant tables and attributes for each component while retaining lookup tables and higher-level relationships needed to interpret them.

The source material includes reduced ER views for the core context as well as larger schema definitions. This allows the discussion to move between a readable business view and the detailed relational implementation.

Mapping entities and attributes to SID

Prepared mappings from tables and individual attributes to SID domains, aggregate business entities and lower-level classes. A table can contain information belonging to several concepts, so the mapping explains the relevant fields and relationships.

The method distinguishes physical and logical resources and relates operational-support concepts to the appropriate information-model context. Mapping descriptions accompany the diagrams so that the result can be reviewed by people who did not create the model.

Potential gaps or extensions were analysed as modelling questions. They were not treated as proof that the corresponding operational functionality had been implemented.

UML modelling and client-specific extensions

Used the TM Forum UML/XMI model as a reference in the modelling workflow. Selected the relevant top-level entities and lower-level classes, retained their associations and then modelled client-specific extensions.

A worked location example extends the geographic-location concept with zones, sub-zones, zone hierarchies, relationships and associated types. The resulting class model is then related back to an ER representation.

The delivered mapping presentation explains the method and demonstrates its core steps on a practical OSS example. It explicitly separates the general procedure from the subset demonstrated in that example.

Site and location identity across systems

Investigated the lifecycle of UK property identifiers and prepared sequence-diagram material for the route from planning application to property-reference matching and status changes. The purpose was to understand how location information becomes available and is maintained across the organisations involved.

This supported the data-modelling discussion around sites, addresses, coordinates and location relationships. The work is a process and integration analysis; it does not claim development of the external planning or addressing services.

A repeatable mapping workflow

The engineering and modelling artefacts support a structured progression from the current system to a SID-aligned design.

  1. Define the process and break it into the activities to be covered.
  2. Identify the source tables, attributes, reference data and ownership for those activities.
  3. Build a focused ER view of the existing relationships.
  4. Map the relevant concepts and fields to SID entities and classes.
  5. Model client-specific extensions in UML while retaining the reference-model relationships.
  6. Relate the resulting model back to an ER structure and document the integration implications.

Tools and deliverables

Produced mapping descriptions, architecture and ER views, UML/sequence-diagram material and integration prototypes. Used Sparx Enterprise Architect, PlantUML, Lucid and MySQL Workbench alongside SQL and the SID/ODA reference models.

The result was a more explicit connection between operational data, business meaning and the industry model, with artefacts that could be used in architecture discussions and further integration work.
