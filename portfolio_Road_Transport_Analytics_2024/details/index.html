Project context and data sources

AKD Affinis is a reporting and control system for Croatian road passenger and freight transport, supported through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan. The beneficiary is the Croatian Ministry of the Sea, Transport and Infrastructure, with the Agency for Commercial Activities (AKD) as project partner.

Its official description identifies the tachograph data-processing system (SOTAH), the national tachograph-card register and ministry transport registers as source systems. The analytical work connected those records with inspection and supervision needs.

Turning transport questions into analytical requirements

Defined more than 40 indicators covering safety, compliance and operating efficiency. Examples included drivers’ rest periods, speed patterns and vehicle use.

Specified analysis by transport company, individual driver, groups of drivers and fleet, with operational and regulatory filters. Considered different generations of tachograph cards and the need to connect their records with inspections.

Full technical and functional specifications

Prepared infrastructure, processing and other specifications, including the technical and functional documentation and bill of quantities needed for procurement and implementation. The documentation connected the analytical objectives to concrete system components, delivery steps and verification requirements.

Governance and personal-data handling

Specified requirements for data access, ownership, quality and privacy. Addressed the responsibilities needed to operate the platform and to use driver-related data for defined analytical purposes.

Connected access, data quality and operational responsibilities with the processing and infrastructure specifications so that the delivery team had a defined basis for implementation.

Analytical subjects and the questions they support

The design joins tachograph information with inspections and ministry data so that analysis can move between a driver, a vehicle, a transport company and a fleet. Supporting different card generations and delivery structures is part of preparing those records for common analysis.

Analytical areaQuestions represented in the requirements
SafetySpeed patterns and conditions requiring investigation
ComplianceDriver rest periods and links with inspection evidence
Operational efficiencyVehicle use and comparison across fleets or groups of drivers
Search and explorationCompany, individual driver, driver group and fleet filters

Platform components in the technical documentation

Designed for a planned scope exceeding 250 TB, more than 75,000 drivers and 65,000 vehicles.

Specified the responsibilities and interactions of storage, processing, transformations, analytical access and governance. These components formed part of the implementation documentation, with diagrams explaining how the infrastructure and data services fit together.

ResponsibilityComponents covered by the documentation

Architecture diagrams and implementation instructions

Authored explanations of the infrastructure architecture, data lake, ETL/ELT processing, warehouse and analytical platform. Documented the key interactions between these components and the practical steps needed to realise them.

Connected analytical requirements and expected future growth with infrastructure sizing. This gave contractors an explanation of the complete system before the detailed component specifications.

Detailed team tasks and candidate evaluation

Led the process of turning the architecture and project requirements into detailed specifications and tasks for the specialists working on the project. Evaluated candidates and their technical competencies against those responsibilities.

  • Data engineering: source integration, data preparation, ETL/ELT, storage and analytical-model requirements.
  • DevOps and platform engineering: infrastructure, test and production environments, deployment and operational requirements.
  • Analytics: indicators, reporting and visualisation requirements connected to the underlying data and processing.
  • Cross-team coordination: clarify responsibilities and dependencies between the platform, engineering and analytics work so that the project could be implemented coherently.

Procurement and implementation support

Prepared technical RFI/RFP inputs, hardware requirements and procurement documentation. Reviewed supplier offers and answered technical questions during market research and procurement.

Provided technical clarifications for the Central Finance and Contracting Agency for EU Programmes and Projects (SAFU). Connected procurement requirements with implementation and verification requirements, with subsequent advisory involvement.

The full technical documentation was used for later project implementation. The 250 TB+, 75,000+ drivers and 65,000+ vehicles describe the sizing and coverage used in the architecture.
