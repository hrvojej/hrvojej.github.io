National transport data and analytical requirements

AKD Affinis connects road-transport records for reporting, supervision and analysis. Its public description identifies the tachograph data-processing system (SOTAH), the national tachograph-card register and registers maintained by the Croatian Ministry of the Sea, Transport and Infrastructure as data sources.

Defined more than 40 safety, compliance and efficiency indicators, including drivers’ rest periods, speed patterns and vehicle use. Specified analysis by driver, carrier, driver group and fleet, connecting tachograph data with inspections and ministry information.

Technical documentation used for implementation

Prepared infrastructure, processing and other specifications as part of the full technical documentation on which the project was subsequently implemented. The work covered test and production environments, hardware, data storage, ETL/ELT, analytical access, governance, procurement and verification requirements.

Authored explanations and diagrams of the infrastructure, data lake, warehouse, processing and analytical components, their interactions and the steps contractors needed to follow.

The documented architecture covered Spark, MinIO, dbt, Dagster, OpenMetadata, PostgreSQL, Trino, StarRocks, Apache Superset and Kubernetes. The 250 TB+, driver and vehicle figures describe the design scope.

Team specifications and candidate evaluation

Led the detailed specification and task-definition process for data engineers, DevOps and analytics specialists. Defined what each role needed to deliver and aligned the responsibilities of the platform, engineering and analytical teams.

Evaluated candidates and technical competencies against those responsibilities, supporting the team setup and subsequent implementation.

Procurement and technical delivery support

Prepared technical RFI/RFP inputs and supported the procurement process through technical requirements, hardware and supplier-offer reviews, and answers to bidders.

Connected the implementation requirements with verification criteria and provided technical clarifications for the Central Finance and Contracting Agency for EU Programmes and Projects (SAFU), alongside subsequent advisory support.
