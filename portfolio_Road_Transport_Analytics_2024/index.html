From heterogeneous transport records to useful indicators

The analytical scope combined digital tachograph records, inspection evidence and ministry data. Designed support for different generations of tachograph cards and for investigation at carrier, driver, group and fleet level.

Defined more than 40 safety, regulatory-compliance and efficiency indicators, including driver rest, speed patterns and vehicle use. Connected these views to inspection needs and anomaly investigation.

Architecture, capacity and platform choices

Designed the open lakehouse for a planned scope above 250 TB, covering more than 75,000 drivers and 65,000 vehicles.

The proposed architecture used MinIO for object storage, Spark for processing, dbt for transformation patterns, Dagster for orchestration, OpenMetadata for metadata/governance, and PostgreSQL alongside StarRocks and Superset for data access and analysis.

Included Kubernetes in the platform design, developed test and production infrastructure requirements, and considered architectural options for future growth towards petabyte scale.

Governance, procurement and delivery preparation

Prepared processing, infrastructure and hardware specifications and technical RFI/RFP inputs under the EU-funded project framework. Contributed to the technical procurement process and collaboration with the selected partner.

Translated personal-data protection and GDPR requirements into architecture and governance considerations. Connected ownership, access, data quality and platform operations to the responsibilities of delivery teams.

Reviewed technical competencies needed across DevOps, Data Engineering and analytics. Aligned infrastructure decisions with implementation needs and the analytical products expected by stakeholders.

Architecture as a basis for implementation

Delivered the architecture, analytical definitions and technical specifications needed to guide platform procurement and implementation. Connected infrastructure capacity, data governance and team responsibilities to the planned analytical services.
