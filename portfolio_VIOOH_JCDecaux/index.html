SQL and dbt engineering for real-time advertising data

Developed and maintained SQL/dbt models that converted complex advertising records into data for analysis in Snowflake.

Client / context
VIOOH / JCDecaux · via Intuita Consulting, UK
Period
During the Intuita engagement · August 2022 – February 2024
My role
Data Engineer / Data Engineering Analyst
1–5 million
nested JSON records per hour in the pipeline
Key contribution

Led model upgrades for a pipeline handling approximately one to five million nested JSON records per hour. Traced faulty records through Kafka, S3, Airflow and Snowflake to identify where processing went wrong.

Context and data flow

The wider real-time bidding environment included Java applications, Kafka streaming and a Kafka sink to S3. Airflow managed loading into Snowflake, where SQL/dbt models prepared data for analytics.

Focused on warehouse transformations, data models and the movement of records through the pipeline, tracing how source data reached analytical outputs.

Engineering contribution

Led upgrades and ongoing maintenance of key SQL models. Worked with nested, multi-source records and the business structures of advertising demand-side and supply-side systems.

Traced transfers and transformations across pipeline stages. Diagnosed malformed or unexpected records and their effects on later processing, linking source behaviour to warehouse outputs.

Operational visibility

Used DataDog and Grafana for monitoring in an environment also using Athena and Lambda. QuickSight and ThoughtSpot were part of the analytical context.

A connected but distinct contribution was the cloud anomaly-detection and notification application described in its own project case study.
