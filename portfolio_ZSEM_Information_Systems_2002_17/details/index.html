Business-school context

Zagreb School of Economics and Management is a private business school specialising in economics and management. It received accreditation from the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB) in 2013, during this engagement. ZŠEM describes its AACSB standing as placing it among the top 6% of business schools worldwide.

Its MBA programme was ranked among the top 200 worldwide and top 60 in Europe in the QS Global MBA Rankings 2020.

Learning platforms over several generations

Implemented and administered WebCT and subsequently Blackboard, supporting approximately 1,500 students, more than 100 lecturers and over ten blended or distance-learning courses.

Prepared Moodle for the 2015 transition from Blackboard, created video guidance and delivered lecturer workshops. Also led programming and testing during development of a MOOC portal.

Email, collaboration and CRM migration

Tested and introduced Google Apps for Education in 2012 and migrated student and guest email/files for approximately 2,500 accounts in 2013. Served as principal administrator of the institutional domain.

Led the implementation and administration of Salesforce, including training and the transition of PR, marketing, MBA and Business Academy teams onto the service.

ERP and virtual teaching environments

Installed and maintained SAP for educational use. Tested and configured Microsoft Dynamics/Navision servers and clients and supported student access and practical exercises.

Prepared virtual teaching environments and images, worked with the University of Zagreb University Computing Centre (SRCE) Virtual Computing Lab, and set up Corporate Planning Suite server/client access with learner guidance.

Presented this work at the University of Zagreb University Computing Centre (SRCE) e-Infrastructure Day on 3 December 2014. The presentation described a Virtual Computing Lab environment for Corporate Planning Suite exercises in the MBA Accounting Information Systems course, and a concept for Microsoft Dynamics exercises in the MBA Enterprise Resource Planning Systems course.

Web infrastructure and digital marketing

Led a web-server upgrade and consolidation of 25 institutional websites into five. Administered institutional sites and approximately 30 student WordPress installations, and supported more than 40 teaching web applications.

Configured Linux hosting with nginx, MySQL, FTP and email. Prepared automated CMS installation and delivered SEO, content-management, web-optimisation and Google AdWords training. The wider work included CMS training and advice on cloud services.

Connected practical website development and maintenance with web analytics and digital marketing. Tools used and taught included Google Analytics, Google Ads (AdWords), Google AdSense, Google Search Console (Webmaster Tools) and Facebook marketing. Independent website projects also provided practical experience for teaching these topics.

Applications and business-system integration

Led development of an administrative and student-services system and worked across HR, CRM, LMS, ERP, web and email. Held an information-security leadership role and developed web applications, services and migrations.

Led the IT part of a courseware/e-book shop, including Adobe Content Server setup and testing. Designed a C#/SOAP/XML product-search integration combining Amazon, eBay, image search and other sources in one research interface.

Doctoral integration research

Built a proof-of-concept framework connecting Blackboard/Moodle with FEDORA, DSpace, Google Docs and desktop context. Researched discovery, interoperability, reuse and exchange of learning and digital objects.

This work formed the basis of the Applied Computer Science doctorate and related publications, establishing a foundation in metadata and integration before the later data-platform projects.

Undergraduate teaching in economics and management

From 2002 to 2017, taught computing and information-systems subjects to domestic and international students at the Zagreb School of Economics and Management (ZŠEM), extensively in English. Adapted technical material to students with different backgrounds and connected it with practical work in business information systems.

  • Web Design — practical web design, web development and work with content management systems.
  • Electronic Business — e-business concepts and practical use of information systems.
  • Information and Communication Technology — foundations and applications of ICT.
  • Management of Information Systems — the role and management of information systems in organisations.
  • Electronic Marketing — e-marketing teaching for business students.
  • Introduction to Software Project Management — teaching and assistance, including technical preparation for international videoconference teaching.

MBA teaching

Taught subjects connecting digital technologies with business and management decisions at MBA level.

Also taught advanced Excel methods to MBA Management and Executive MBA participants, connecting data analysis and visualisation with practical business tasks.

  • Electronic Commerce Management.
  • Electronic Business Management.
  • Web Management — including web design and e-marketing content.

Curriculum development

Contributed to the development of e-business curricula for MBA Management and MBA Management – HRM in 2012, worked on the Web Management curriculum for MBA Management of Information Systems in 2014, and developed an E-marketing elective in 2015.

Professional education and practical workshops

Delivered workshops and seminars on Moodle, Joomla, search-engine optimisation, content marketing, website optimisation and Google AdWords. These activities connected practical technical skills with the needs of lecturers, website editors and business users.

Delivered Open Source Courseware workshops at seven locations in five South African cities in 2014, and a Moodle/MOOC workshop in Linz in 2015.

Conference participation and thesis supervision

Represented ZŠEM at Croatia Meeting G2 in 2016 as a panellist discussing whether Croatian higher-education institutions were oriented towards global markets.

Participated in the MIPRO 2016 conference in Opatija, held from 31 May to 3 June.

Supervised a thesis examining the future of e-commerce through Alibaba’s growth, connecting business education with digital commerce.

Advanced Excel seminars for business professionals

Delivered practical advanced Excel seminars for business users, helping participants analyse, check and present their own business data. One documented example is the ZŠEM Business Academy seminar held on 6–7 February 2017.

Training materials combined an Advanced Chapters guide with workbooks for practical exercises. Topics also included database functions in Google Sheets and techniques for importing and extracting web data.

  • Pivot tables, pivot charts and what-if analysis.
  • Lookup and reference functions: VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, INDEX and MATCH, with data validation.
  • Text preparation, logical functions and nested IF statements.
  • Sorting, filtering, conditional formatting and database functions.

E-marketing and market research for business users

Delivered a practical e-marketing seminar for business professionals through ZŠEM Business Academy in 2015, focused on researching markets, trends and target audiences. Used examples from different industries to connect the tools with marketing-campaign planning.

  • Researching market segments, niches, audience interests and changes in trends.
  • Using Facebook for market and audience analysis.
  • Using Google Keyword Planner to research keywords and compare their popularity.

CARNET E-Learning Academy tutoring

From October 2005 to October 2008, tutored professors and teachers at the Croatian Academic and Research Network (CARNET) E-Learning Academy, helping them develop online courses.

  • E-learning administrators.
  • E-learning tutors.
  • E-learning course designers.

Further professional education for business and IT users

Professional education covered the practical use of analytical tools, business information systems and online services.

  • Advanced analytics using Microsoft Power BI and PowerPivot.
  • Selecting, implementing and integrating human-resources information systems; gamification in human resources.
  • Customer relationship management (CRM) systems.
  • Google AdWords and Facebook marketing; website and search-engine optimisation.
  • Mobile and IT security.
  • Virtual infrastructure for business projects.
