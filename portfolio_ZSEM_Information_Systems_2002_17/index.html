Learning platforms and the move to Moodle

Implemented and maintained distance-learning environments from 2002, beginning with WebCT and subsequently Blackboard. Administered services for approximately 1,500 students and more than 100 lecturers, with support for over ten blended/distance courses.

In 2015, set up and administered Moodle for migration from Blackboard, developed video guidance and delivered lecturer workshops. Also led programming and testing during the development phase of a MOOC portal.

Google Apps and Salesforce migrations

Tested and introduced Google Apps for Education in 2012, followed by migration of student and guest email/files for approximately 2,500 accounts in 2013. Served as the principal administrator for the institutional domain.

Led Salesforce implementation and administration in 2013. Prepared seminars, training and the move of PR, marketing, MBA and Business Academy teams onto the cloud service.

ERP and virtual teaching laboratories

Installed, administered and maintained SAP for educational use. Tested and configured Microsoft Dynamics/Navision servers and clients, managed access and supported practical student work.

Set up virtual/cloud teaching environments and worked with SRCE’s Virtual Computing Lab. Prepared virtual images and Corporate Planning Suite server/client access, with video instructions for learners.

Web infrastructure and consolidation

Led a 2016 web-server upgrade and consolidation of 25 sites into five principal institutional websites. Administered and optimised institutional sites and a separate estate of approximately 30 student WordPress installations.

Configured virtual Linux hosting with nginx, MySQL, FTP and email services. Prepared automated installation of open-source CMS applications for course web accounts and supported more than 40 web applications for teaching.

Provided SEO, editorial/CMS training and advice on cloud services. The work joined infrastructure administration with the practical needs of lecturers, editors and students.

Application and integration projects

Led development of an administrative/student-services system in 2007 and worked across student administration, HR, CRM, LMS, ERP, web and email. Held an information-security leadership role and developed web applications, services and migrations.

Led the IT part of the MATE courseware/e-book shop project in 2015, including Adobe Content Server installation, testing and trial implementation.

Designed and led a product-search integration using C#, SOAP services and XML to bring results from Amazon, eBay, image search and other sources into one research interface. The system was in production in 2014.

Doctoral integration research

Built a 2012 proof-of-concept framework connecting Blackboard/Moodle with FEDORA, DSpace, Google Docs and the user’s desktop context. Researched discovery, interoperability, reuse and exchange of learning/digital objects.

This work underpinned the Applied Computer Science doctorate and related publications, providing an earlier foundation for later data-platform and metadata architecture.

Teaching and knowledge transfer

Taught domestic and international students, extensively in English. Topics included Web Design, Electronic Business, ICT, Management of Information Systems, Electronic Marketing and software-project-management teaching support, alongside MBA e-commerce, e-business and web-management courses.

Developed curricula and delivered professional seminars in CMS/LMS adoption, analytics, web optimisation and marketing systems. Supported more than 50 videoconference lectures/presentations.

Related educational work included CARNET e-learning tutoring, 2005–2008; Open Source Courseware workshops at seven locations in five South African cities in 2014; and a Moodle/MOOC workshop in Linz in 2015.
